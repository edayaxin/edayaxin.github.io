I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science with a minor in Communication Arts at UW-Madison advised by Prof. Bilge Mutlu. My research lies at the intersection of human robot interaction (HRI), embodied AI, and accessibility. I study intelligent robots that support people's inclusive engagement with the social world and the physical world, and understand how these robots can be meaningfully integrated into human environments.

Robot-Assisted Group Tours for Blind People
CHI 2026 paper
An AI-powered mobile robot supports blind people to join group tours with sighted visitors. The robot can provide contextual information and directions of nearby people, notify the guide and support navigation to follow the group.
Remote Robot for Place Attachment
HRI 2025 paper | video
Multi-party interactions in robotic telepresence to promote place attachment. Users were guided by a human guide or a virtual agent, and experienced the environment individually or with other users.
NarraGuide: an LLM-based Mobile Robot
UIST 2025 paper | video
A fully autonomous robotic system that provides LLM-powered narrative guidance for users to explore a remote geology museum through a dialogue-based interface.
Conversational Robot for Homebound Older Adults
DIS 2024 paper | video
Explore the design and use of conversational telepresence robots to help homebound older adults interact with the external world.
SMART-TBI
ASSETS 2024 paper
Social Media Accessibility and Rehabilitation Toolkit for people with traumatic brain injury (SMART-TBI). The toolkit was designed to address the cognitive and communicative needs of individuals with TBI.
Understanding Social Media Use by People with TBI
CHI 2023 paper
Conceptualize tools to address challenges in accessing Facebook with 10 adults with TBI through a participatory design study.
Conversational Agent for People with TBI
ASSETS 2023 paper
Study TBI-related use of conversatioanl agents through a four-week at-home study to investigate use patterns, challenges, and design requirements.
Politeness Conversation Design for Older Adults
CHI 2022 paper | video
Designed conversational agents on for older adults based on politeness theory. Field studies revealed user personas with varied preferences in system politeness and user agency in the interaction.
Social Robot for Collaborative Creative Activities
DIS 2021 paper | video
A mobile robot with social expressions collaborated with human in a creative activity where the robot and human took turns to create compositions using tangram pieces in an augmented projection space.
